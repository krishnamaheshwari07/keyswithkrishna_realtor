# Keys with Krishna brand colours (sampled from the logo, Oct 17 2026)

Use these for every post, carousel, Reel, Story, cover and video end card. They replace the old dark forest green / cream / old gold.

- Deep teal  #032b38 (text on white, base of dark theme)
- Teal       #04566b / #02495f (cards, gradient start)
- Green      #00784e (gradient middle)
- Lime       #6ebe28 (gradient accent, use sparingly, never behind small text)
- Gold       #fac951 (pills, rules, highlights)
- Navy       #082035 (suit navy; text on gold)
- White      #ffffff

Two looks, alternated as before:
- DARK: teal -> green gradient background (135deg, #032b38 > #02495f > #00784e), white text, gold accents.
- LIGHT (formerly "cream"): white background, deep teal text, gold accents, thin teal->green->lime bar across the top.

The templates in templates/ already use these values; keep their CSS variables.
