Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Who we are

Keys with Krishna is the real estate marketing brand of Krishna Rander, Realtor®, CENTURY 21 PROPERTY ZONE REALTY INC., Brokerage, Ontario, Canada. This policy covers the Keys with Krishna publishing app, which posts our own educational and market content to the Keys with Krishna Instagram account, Facebook Page and TikTok account.

What the app does

The app connects only to accounts we own. It publishes posts, Reels, Stories and carousels that we create, and reads basic performance statistics (such as reach and engagement totals) for our own posts so we can improve our content.

Information we collect

  • The app does not collect personal information from people who view our posts.
  • When you comment on a post or send us a message, that information is held by Meta (Instagram and Facebook) under Meta's own privacy policy. Automated replies to comments are sent through Meta's tools.
  • If you contact us directly by phone, message or email, we use your details only to answer your enquiry and provide real estate services you ask for.

How we use and share information

We use information only to respond to you and to provide real estate services. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We handle personal information in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and the obligations of a registered real estate salesperson in Ontario.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to serve you or as required by law and brokerage record-keeping rules, then delete them.

Data deletion

To ask us to delete any information we hold about you, call or email us with your name and the account or phone number you contacted us from. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep a record. You can also delete your own comments and messages at any time in Instagram or Facebook.
